Vision & Leadership

To Be Added, Founder and Chairman of the Shree GURUS Group of Institutions, is a visionary educationist who has profoundly shaped the educational landscape of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Rising from humble beginnings, he transformed educational gaps into a lifelong mission of making quality education accessible to every student.

His philosophy of holistic learning — shaping the mind, character, and spirit of every student — has redefined education, fostering not only academic excellence but also resilience, creativity, and transformative personal growth.

Through his relentless dedication, he has shaped countless leaders across every industry, given life-changing opportunities to many families and communities, and inspired educators. Today, under his leadership, Shree GURUS has grown from a modest tutorial center into one of the state's largest educational hubs, spanning schools, colleges, and a deemed university, serving over 90,000 students nationwide.

Profile Summary

A visionary educationist with over four decades of dedicated service in shaping young minds, To Be Added has played a pivotal role in transforming the educational landscape of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Coming from an agricultural background, he embodies the values of perseverance, discipline, and empathy.

Passionate about learning and public service, Dr. Rathaiah often says he has three great loves in life — journalism, politics, and education. His commitment to each has shaped his multifaceted approach to educational leadership and societal transformation.

Genesis of Shree GURUS Group of Institutions

To Be Added founded his first educational institution in 1979 at the age of 27, driven by a desire to address the challenges faced by rural youth who often dropped out due to lack of guidance. His childhood goal of educating a few around him grew into a transformative mission.

Key Contributions & Leadership

Innovative Educational Leadership

Dr. Rathaiah pioneered the model residential intermediate college at Vadlamudi, Guntur district, combining the best practices of Indian and modern education systems. He consistently applied research in educational psychology, student-centric pedagogy, and adolescent learning, producing generations of successful engineers, doctors, lawyers, technocrats, and entrepreneurs.

Socio-Economic Impact

By focusing on holistic student development and academic excellence, Dr. Rathaiah has significantly contributed to regional socio-economic development. His innovative teaching methods, coupled with mentoring and remedial support for rural youth, have transformed the lives of thousands of students.

Focus on Learning Environment

A hallmark of all Shree GURUS institutions is their location in serene, natural surroundings, reflecting Dr. Rathaiah's belief in the importance of environment on learning and creativity. Every facility—from hostels and sports amenities to classrooms—is designed to ensure a stimulating, safe, and nurturing environment.

Holistic Development & Academic Excellence

Dr. Rathaiah believes that strong academics and holistic growth must go hand in hand. Shree GURUS institutions ensure academic rigor, wholesome nutrition, sports, and personality development. Through Shree GURUS's Publications, he has contributed to quality educational resources for competitive exams.

Redefining Education, Steering It Towards a New Horizon

Education has always been the cornerstone of progress — guiding societies through challenges toward hope, renewal, and transformation. Foreseeing this truth four decades ago, To Be Added, Founder and Chairman of the Shree GURUS Group of Institutions, emerged as a pioneering force in Indian education. A visionary reformer and champion of value-based learning, he redefined what it means to be a responsible educationist — blending academic excellence with character, compassion, and social responsibility.

From the very beginning, Dr. Rathaiah viewed education not as a commercial pursuit but as a mission for national development and social upliftment. Moved by the challenges faced by talented students from rural backgrounds, he envisioned a holistic residential education model that nurtures intellectual, emotional, and physical well-being — a philosophy that continues to shape Shree GURUS's identity today.

Under his leadership, Shree GURUS has grown from a single institution into a comprehensive educational ecosystem spanning schools, colleges, and a Deemed-to-be University, serving over 90,000 students nationwide. Guided by the principle of "Technology with a Human Face," Shree GURUS integrates innovation with empathy, ethics, and social engagement.

The institution emphasizes emotional counselling, mentorship, fitness, and mindfulness, ensuring holistic growth and psychological well-being — earning its distinction as a "zero-depression campus." With a strong focus on industry-academia collaboration, Dr. Rathaiah introduced pioneering initiatives such as Faculty Industry Immersion Programs, skill development modules, and practice-oriented curricula.

Beyond academics, Shree GURUS instills empathy and leadership through community engagement, encouraging students to visit orphanages, old-age homes, and participate in relief efforts during natural calamities — fostering the spirit of service that defines true education.

Today, under his stewardship, Shree GURUS continues to expand with upcoming campuses in Bengaluru and Mumbai, marking a new era of growth, innovation, and inclusivity.

A trailblazer in higher education, To Be Added has transformed education into a movement for national progress — redefining learning not merely as the pursuit of knowledge but as the art of building character, competence, and compassion.

A Trailblazer in Education

From humble beginnings, Dr. Rathaiah has emerged as a role model for aspiring edupreneurs, advocating that educationists should be leaders and change agents for society. He emphasizes teacher training, innovative pedagogy, and ICT in education to raise standards and prepare India for globalization.

Known for his work ethic, vision, and commitment, To Be Added continues to inspire educators and students alike, shaping the future of education in India and transforming lives through the power of learning.
