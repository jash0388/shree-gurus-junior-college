Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about admissions, academics, and campus life

Admission is based on 10th grade marks and performance in our entrance test. The process includes:

  1. Submit online application with 10th marks memo
  2. Appear for Shree GURUS Entrance Test (VET)
  3. Personal interview with faculty
  4. Document verification and fee payment

Annual fees range from ₹85,000 for MPC with IIT-JEE to ₹73,000 for regular BIPC. This includes tuition, study material, test series, and lab charges. Transport and hostel fees are additional. We offer merit-based scholarships up to 100% fee waiver.

We provide separate hostels for boys and girls with:

  • AC and non-AC rooms with attached bathrooms
  • 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance
  • Wi-Fi connectivity in study areas
  • Separate study rooms with individual desks
  • Common rooms with TV and indoor games
  • Regular health check-ups
  • Nutritious meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)

We follow an integrated approach:

  • Curriculum designed to cover both board syllabus and competitive exam patterns
  • Regular tests for both boards and entrances
  • Dedicated doubt-clearing sessions
  • Special weekend classes for advanced topics
  • Performance analysis and personalized feedback
  • Previous year question papers practice

We operate a fleet of GPS-tracked AC buses covering all major areas of Hyderabad including Kukatpally, Miyapur, Bachupally, KPHB, Kondapur, Gachibowli, and surrounding areas. Each bus has a lady attendant and first aid kit for student safety.

We offer a wide range of extracurricular activities:

  • Sports: Cricket, Basketball, Tennis, Badminton, Athletics
  • Cultural: Music, Dance, Drama, Art & Craft
  • Clubs: Science Club, Literary Club, Eco Club
  • Events: Annual Day, Sports Day, Science Exhibition
  • Personality Development: Public speaking, Leadership training
