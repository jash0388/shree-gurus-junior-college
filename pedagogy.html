PEDAGOGY

Top ranks are not won by solving 1,000 random questions. They are won by eliminating repeat mistakes. Here is how Sri Guru Brahmam's 3-Tier System guarantees mastery.

01

Daily Micro Diagnostic

Immediately following every lecture block, students solve a timed 15-minute diagnostic test testing the precise conceptual mechanics just taught. Immediate doubt resolution occurs on the spot.

02

Weekend Grand Tests

Every Sunday, full-length simulations are conducted under strict NTA / JEE CBT conditions. AI-generated negative marking heatmaps identify question-skipping efficiency and stress patterns.

03

Individual Error Log

Every wrong question must be hand-logged into the student's Error Diary and re-solved with a senior faculty mentor before Monday morning lectures begin. Repeat mistakes drop by 88%.
