Shree GURUS Group of Institutions offers two main streams: MPC (Maths, Physics, Chemistry) for IIT-JEE preparation and BiPC (Biology, Physics, Chemistry) for NEET preparation. We also provide specialized programs including SAT coaching for global studies and foundation courses for younger students.

Our faculty members are highly qualified experts with proven track records in IIT-JEE and NEET preparation. Many have directly mentored thousands of successful students who are now at top engineering and medical colleges. They bring both academic excellence and practical coaching experience to every class.
