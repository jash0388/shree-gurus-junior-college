Shree GURUS – A Pioneer in Residential Junior College Education

Shree GURUS is widely recognised as the first private residential junior college in the Telugu states, setting benchmarks in quality education for more than a decade. Over the years, Shree GURUS students have consistently secured hundreds of top ranks in Intermediate, IIT-JEE, and NEET, firmly establishing the institution as a path-setter in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

As the education sector gradually witnessed unhealthy practices, excessive publicity, and commercialization in the name of ranks, academic standards began to decline and core values weakened. At Shree GURUS, we made a conscious and principled decision not to compromise on educational integrity. With this conviction, the institution adopted a measured and disciplined approach toward expansion at the +2 level, prioritising quality over scale and long-term student success over short-term visibility.

Our Vision

To evolve into a centre of excellence in Science & Technology through creative and innovative practices in teaching - learning, towards promoting academic achievement and research excellence to produce internationally accepted, competitive and world class professionals who are psychologically strong & emotionally balanced imbued with social consciousness & ethical values.

Our Mission

To provide high quality academic programmes, training activities, research facilities and opportunities supported by continuous industry - institute interaction aimed at promoting employability, entrepreneurship, leadership and research aptitude among students and contribute to the economic and technological development of the region, state and nation.

Our Commitment

Academic excellence and ethical practices; holistic student development; discipline, integrity, and accountability; innovation and research orientation; and social responsibility and community impact.
