Introduction

At Shree GURUS Group of Institutions, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit or interact with our website shreegurus.edu.in. By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information You Provide:

  • Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number
  • Academic details and Contact Information
  • Any additional information submitted via forms (admissions, brochure requests, enquiries)

Technical / Usage Data:

  • IP address, Browser type, and Operating system
  • Pages visited, Date and time of access, and Time spent on pages

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for purposes including but not limited to:

  • Responding to enquiries, applications, and brochure requests
  • Processing admissions and administrative activities
  • Providing updates about college programs, events, and announcements
  • Improving our website and user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with authorized college staff, service providers assisting with website functionality, and legal authorities if required by law. All third parties are obligated to protect your information.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

To enhance user experience, we may use cookies and similar technologies to collect usage data and remember settings. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect certain website features.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access. However, no internet-based data transfer can be guaranteed as 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy and as required by law. After that, we securely delete or anonymize your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Correct or update your information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Please check back periodically for any revisions.
