Motivational Environment and Peer Learning

Holistic Growth

FAQs for Intermediate MPC with IIT-JEE

If you are aiming to make a career in medicine or life science by securing a spot in a top Medical college in India, a standard BiPC course may not be enough. Our BiPC + NEET Integrated program at Shree GURUS builds a strong foundation for these fields, which can prepare you for both the Intermediate board and the NEET exams. This balanced approach ensures that you can perform well in both, giving your best shot at securing admissions to leading medical institutions.

The integrated BiPC + NEET course at Shree GURUS Group of Institutions combines the Intermediate board syllabus with NEET preparation. This ensures that students prepare for both their board exams and NEET in a streamlined manner, without juggling between separate coaching classes.

The curriculum covers Intermediate subjects (Biology, Physics, and Chemistry) for board exams and also focuses on the in-depth study required for NEET, including extensive practice in solving MCQs and mock tests. The syllabus of BiPC is as prescribed by the Telangana Board of Intermediate Education.

Balancing both board exams and NEET requires consistent revision, regular practice tests, and smart time-management. With our BiPC + NEET Integrated program, you can learn the board syllabus alongside NEET preparation, streamlining your study process. This not only saves time but also ensures you can revise regularly and stay on track with practice tests, boosting your overall performance.

Biology: Human Physiology, Plant Physiology, Genetics, Ecology, and Cell Biology.

Physics: Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Electrodynamics, and Optics.

Chemistry: Organic Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, and Physical Chemistry.

The subjects and topics covered in BiPC are as prescribed by the Telangana Board of Intermediate Education.

Studying and preparing with an integrated program saves time and ensures comprehensive preparation for both the board exams and NEET. Since the curriculum is synchronized, it is easier for students to focus on both without feeling overwhelmed. With maximized study time and regular revisions, students can excel in both exams.
